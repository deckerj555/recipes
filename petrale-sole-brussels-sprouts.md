# Petrale Sole Meunière with Roasted Brussels Sprouts

*Delicate sole in nutty brown butter and lemon, with crisp, caramelized Brussels sprouts. Four main ingredients.*

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided
- 2 lemons (1 zested and juiced, 1 cut into wedges)
- 1¾ lb petrale sole fillets (2 large Tre-Fin fillets), cut into 4 portions
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ cup all-purpose flour, for dredging
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley (optional)

## Steps

1. **Roast the sprouts.** Heat the oven to 425°F with a sheet pan inside. Toss the Brussels sprouts with 2 tbsp of the olive oil and 1 tsp of the salt. Spread them on the hot pan cut-side down and roast until deeply browned and tender. Start the fish when they have about 10 minutes left. *(timer: 22 min)*
2. **Season and dredge.** Pat the sole very dry. Season both sides with the remaining ½ tsp of salt and black pepper. Dredge in the flour and shake off the excess so only a thin film is left.
3. **Sear the first side.** Heat the remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil with 2 tbsp of the butter in a 12-inch nonstick or carbon-steel skillet over medium-high until the foaming subsides. Lay in the fillets (work in 2 batches if crowded) and cook without moving until golden. *(timer: 2 min 30 sec)*
4. **Flip and finish.** Flip carefully with a thin fish spatula and cook until just opaque. Petrale is thin and cooks fast. Move the fish to warm plates. *(timer: 1 min)*
5. **Brown the butter.** Wipe out the skillet and add the remaining 4 tbsp of butter over medium heat. Swirl until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn hazelnut-brown, then take it off the heat right away. *(timer: 2 min 30 sec)*
6. **Sauce and serve.** Swirl the juice of the zested lemon from the lemons into the brown butter (it will sputter) and spoon it over the fish. Toss the sprouts with the lemon zest. Scatter parsley over the fish if using, and serve with lemon wedges.
