# Mild Chicken Chile Verde

*Salsa verde braise with all the roasted tomatillo flavor, none of the burn — poblanos only, no hot chilis*

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 3 poblano peppers
- 1 yellow onion, quartered
- 6 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 1 bunch cilantro, stems and all
- 2½ lb chicken whole legs (or thighs)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp Habanada chili powder (optional, for flavor without heat)
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1 tbsp neutral oil or lard
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 lime, juiced

## Steps

1. **Char the vegetables.** Set a rack near the broiler. Arrange tomatillos, poblano peppers, yellow onion, and garlic cloves on a foil-lined sheet pan. Broil, turning once, until blackened in spots and the tomatillos are soft and leaking juice, 8–12 minutes. Pull the garlic early if it's browning fast. *(timer: 10 min)*
2. **Steam and peel the poblanos.** Transfer the poblanos to a bowl, cover, and let them steam for 10 minutes. Slip off the charred skins, then remove stems and seeds — the seeds and ribs are where any residual bite lives, so be thorough. *(timer: 10 min)*
3. **Blend the salsa verde.** Peel the garlic. Blend the tomatillos (with their juices), poblanos, onion, garlic, and cilantro until mostly smooth. Taste — it should be bright and tangy with no heat.
4. **Brown the chicken.** Season chicken with kosher salt. Heat neutral oil or lard in a Dutch oven over medium-high and brown the chicken skin-side down until deeply golden, about 6 minutes; flip and brown 3 more. Work in batches if needed. Pour off all but a film of fat. *(timer: 9 min)*
5. **Bloom the spices and braise.** Add ground cumin and Habanada chili powder to the pot and stir 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the salsa verde and chicken stock, scrape up the fond, and nestle the chicken back in skin-side up. Simmer, partially covered, until the chicken is fall-apart tender, 45–50 minutes. *(timer: 45 min)*
6. **Finish.** Pull the chicken; shred off the bone if you like, or serve whole legs. Simmer the sauce uncovered a few minutes if it needs thickening, then stir in lime juice and season with salt. Return the chicken to the sauce. Serve with rice, warm tortillas, and quick-pickled radishes. *(timer: 5 min)*

## Notes

Everything but pantry staples is on the current market list: tomatillos (Killer Tomato), poblanos (Feather Oak), chicken legs (Tail Feather Farm), Habanada powder (Hayshaker). If you want a touch more depth with still-zero heat, char one Habanada or sweet banana pepper alongside the poblanos. Leftovers make excellent enchiladas suizas.
