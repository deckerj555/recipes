# Kenji's Ski House Soup

*Kenji López-Alt's spinach and chickpea stew: tomatoes puréed with ginger, smoked paprika, a big pot of greens and chickpeas simmered thick — about 1½ hours, mostly unattended simmering, serve with crusty bread*

<!-- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DWB1EOLw_s -->

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup olive oil, plus 2 tbsp to finish
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced (about 8 thin coins)
- 1 can (28 oz) whole tomatoes, half puréed with the ginger, half chopped
- 1 can (28 oz) chickpeas, with their liquid
- 12 oz spinach, torn (or dino kale)
- 1 tsp sweet smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- ½ tsp MSG (optional)
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar or white wine vinegar
- Crusty bread, for serving

## Steps

1. **Purée the tomatoes.** Blend half the tomatoes and their juice with the ginger until smooth. Chop the rest.
2. **Sweat the onion.** Heat the ⅓ cup oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion until translucent. *(timer: 4 min)* Add the garlic and cook until fragrant. *(timer: 1 min)*
3. **Bloom the paprika.** Stir in the paprika, salt and MSG. Toasting it in the oil for a moment brings out the smoke without scorching it. *(timer: 30 sec)*
4. **Add the tomatoes.** Pour in the purée and bring to a gentle simmer, then add the chopped tomatoes. *(timer: 4 min)*
5. **Wilt the greens.** Add the spinach in two batches, letting the first wilt before adding the second. *(timer: 3 min)* If using dino kale, strip the stems and give it a few minutes longer.
6. **Add the chickpeas.** Stir in the chickpeas with their liquid and the bay leaves. The starchy liquid is what thickens the stew.
7. **Simmer.** Simmer uncovered, stirring now and then, until thick enough to mound on a spoon. With the 28 oz can there's more liquid, so expect the longer end. *(timer: 45 min)*
8. **Finish.** Pull the bay leaves. Taste for salt, then stir in the vinegar and the last 2 tbsp of oil. Serve with crusty bread.

## Notes

Use sweet smoked paprika, not hot; keep it mild for the kids.

A poached egg on top turns it into a fuller dinner.

Dino kale is lacinato; about two bunches gives 12 oz once stemmed.
