# Ham Bone Lentil Soup

*A hearty, deeply flavored soup that transforms a leftover ham bone into something truly comforting. The bone adds richness no store-bought broth can match.*

Stovetop version below; the slow-cooker version is at the end.

## Ingredients

- 1 ham bone (with some meat still on)
- 1½ cups green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup diced tomatoes (canned)
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 fresh thyme sprigs (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 cup leftover diced ham (picked from bone)
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps

1. **Sauté the aromatics.** Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and starting to turn golden, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
2. **Build the base.** Add the tomatoes, smoked paprika, and black pepper, and stir to combine. Cook for 2 minutes to let the tomatoes concentrate slightly.
3. **Add lentils, ham bone, and liquid.** Add the lentils, the ham bone, the broth, the water, bay leaves, and thyme. Stir everything together and bring to a boil over high heat.
4. **Simmer low and slow.** Once boiling, reduce heat to low, partially cover, and simmer until the lentils are completely tender and the broth is rich and flavorful, about 60–75 minutes. Stir occasionally and add a splash of water if the soup gets too thick. *(timer: 1 hr 10 min)*
5. **Remove and shred the bone.** Carefully remove the ham bone and set it on a cutting board. Pull off any remaining meat and shred or chop it into bite-sized pieces. Discard the bone, bay leaves, and thyme sprigs.
6. **Finish the soup.** Return the shredded bone meat to the pot along with the diced ham. Taste and adjust seasoning — the ham is salty, so you likely won't need much added salt. Simmer for another 5–10 minutes to meld flavors. *(timer: 10 min)*
7. **Serve.** Ladle into bowls and top with parsley. Serve with crusty bread for dipping.

## Notes

**Lentil choice:** Green and brown lentils hold their shape well here. Red lentils will break down completely, giving a thicker, creamier texture — also delicious but a different result. Avoid French (Puy) lentils as they take longer and resist absorbing the ham flavor as well.

**Make it thicker:** Use the back of a spoon to mash some lentils against the side of the pot, or blend a cup of the soup and stir it back in.

**Storage:** This soup keeps well in the fridge for 5 days and freezes beautifully for up to 3 months. The flavor actually improves overnight.

## Slow-cooker version

*All the same deep, smoky flavor as the stovetop version — just toss it in and walk away. Great for starting in the morning and coming home to dinner.* Serves 6.

**Changes to the ingredients:** skip the olive oil, use 1 cup water instead of 2, and use 1 tsp dried thyme instead of fresh sprigs. Everything else is the same.

1. **Load the slow cooker.** Add the onion, carrots, celery, garlic, lentils, tomatoes, bay leaves, dried thyme, smoked paprika, and black pepper to the slow cooker. Stir to combine. Nestle the ham bone into the center.
2. **Add liquid.** Pour the broth and water over everything. The liquid should just cover the ingredients — add a splash more water if needed. Put the lid on.
3. **Cook low and slow.** Cook on LOW for 8–10 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours, until the lentils are completely tender and the broth is rich and deeply flavored. *(timer: 9 hr)*
4. **Remove and shred the bone.** Carefully lift out the ham bone and set it on a cutting board. Pull off any remaining meat and shred or chop it. Discard the bone and bay leaves.
5. **Finish and season.** Return the shredded bone meat to the slow cooker along with the diced ham. Stir, taste, and adjust seasoning. Replace the lid and cook on HIGH for another 15 minutes to meld everything together. *(timer: 15 min)*
6. **Serve.** Ladle into bowls and finish with parsley. Serve with crusty bread.

**Skip the sauté:** Unlike many slow cooker recipes, this one doesn't really need a pre-sauté step — the long cook time develops plenty of flavor on its own. If you want a slightly deeper, more caramelized base, you can sauté the onion, carrot, and celery in a pan for 5 minutes before adding, but it's entirely optional.

**Liquid:** Slow cookers trap steam, so you need slightly less liquid than the stovetop version. Don't be tempted to add extra — it will thin out the soup.

**Lentils:** Do not use red lentils in a slow cooker — they turn to mush. Stick with green or brown.

**Timing flexibility:** This soup is very forgiving. If it goes an extra hour on LOW, it'll be fine. Great for busy days.
