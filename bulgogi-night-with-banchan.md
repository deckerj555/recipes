# Bulgogi Night with Banchan

*Marinated grilled beef with quick daikon pickles, napa slaw, and marinade-glazed shiitake*

Uses one batch of [Bulgogi Marinade](bulgogi-marinade.md).

## Ingredients

- 2 lb beef skirt steak or flat iron steak
- 1 batch bulgogi marinade (see Bulgogi Marinade), divided
- 8 oz shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced
- 1 medium daikon radish, peeled and cut into matchsticks
- ½ cup rice vinegar
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp kosher salt (for the pickles)
- ½ head napa cabbage, sliced into thin ribbons
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 4 spring onions or scallions, thinly sliced, divided
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (for the slaw)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (for the slaw)
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 cups short-grain white rice, cooked
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for the pan or grill

## Steps

1. **Make the daikon pickles.** Whisk rice vinegar, sugar, and kosher salt until dissolved. Pack the daikon into a jar, pour the brine over, and refrigerate at least 1 hour (overnight is better). These keep 2 weeks and cut the richness of the beef. *(timer: 1 hr)*
2. **Marinate the beef.** Set aside about 1/3 cup of the bulgogi marinade for the mushrooms. Put the steak in a bag or dish with the rest, turning to coat. Refrigerate 3–4 hours — don't go past 12 or the pear enzymes turn the texture soft. *(timer: 3 hr 30 min)*
3. **Make the napa slaw.** Toss napa cabbage, carrots, and half of spring onions with sesame oil, rice vinegar, and a pinch of salt. Let it sit 15 minutes so it wilts slightly but keeps its crunch. Add half of toasted sesame seeds just before serving. *(timer: 15 min)*
4. **Cook the rice.** Start rice so it's ready when the beef comes off. Keep it covered and warm. *(timer: 20 min)*
5. **Glaze the shiitake.** Heat a skillet over medium-high with a little neutral oil. Sauté shiitake mushrooms until browned and releasing their liquid, 5–6 minutes. Pour in the reserved marinade and cook until it reduces to a glaze that coats the mushrooms, 2–3 minutes. Set aside. *(timer: 8 min)*
6. **Grill the beef.** Get a grill or cast-iron pan ripping hot. Pull the steak from the marinade and let excess drip off — wet meat steams instead of searing. Cook 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, working in batches if the pan is crowded. The sugar in the marinade chars fast, so watch it closely. *(timer: 8 min)*
7. **Rest and slice.** Let the steak rest 5–10 minutes, then slice thinly across the grain. Scatter the remaining spring onions and toasted sesame seeds over the top. *(timer: 8 min)*
8. **Serve.** Set out the sliced beef, rice, glazed shiitake, daikon pickles, and slaw family-style so everyone builds their own plate or lettuce wraps.

## Notes

Reserve about 1/3 cup of the marinade BEFORE the meat goes in — that's what glazes the mushrooms. Never reuse marinade that touched raw beef. Skirt steak: marinate whole 3–4 hours, grill hot and fast, slice across the grain after resting. Flat iron: same approach, slightly thicker so give it an extra minute per side. If you'd rather do the classic paper-thin version, freeze the steak 30–45 minutes and slice it thin before marinating, then cook 2 minutes total in a screaming-hot pan. The daikon pickles want at least an hour but are better the next day — make them first thing, or the night before. Everything but the rice, vinegar, and sugar is on the current market list: skirt/flat iron (Little Seven Seven), shiitake (Columbia Mushroom), daikon and napa (Gargoyle Gardens), carrots, and Asian pears for the marinade.
