# Shio Koji Flank Steak with Honey-Roasted Carrots

*Koji-cured flank steak seared hard in cast iron, with honey-butter roasted carrots. Start the cure 1–3 days ahead.*

## Ingredients

- 1 flank steak, about 2 lb
- 5 tbsp shio koji (about 10% of the meat's weight)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 lb carrots, cut into 3-inch batons
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tbsp honey
- Steamed rice, sliced scallions and lime wedges, for serving

## Steps

1. **Koji rub.** Pat the steak dry and rub the shio koji over every surface. Refrigerate uncovered on a rack over a sheet pan. The koji's enzymes tenderize the meat, and the dry surface sears instead of steaming. *(timer: 1–3 days)*
2. **Temper.** Scrape off any thick koji (it burns fast) and pat dry. No added salt; the koji seasons it. *(timer: 30 min)*
3. **Carrots.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the carrots with the butter, honey and a pinch of salt, and roast, flipping once, until browned at the edges. *(timer: 25 min)*
4. **Sear.** Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat until it just smokes, add the oil, and sear the steak, flipping every minute, until it reads 125°F in the thickest part. Lower the heat to medium-high if the crust darkens too fast. *(timer: 8 min)*
5. **Rest.** *(timer: 10 min)*
6. **Slice and serve.** Slice thin across the grain on a slight angle. Serve over rice with the carrots, scallions and lime.

## Notes

1 day of koji gives a light effect; 2–3 days gives the full dry-aged texture. Don't go past 3 days.
