# Kenji's Weeknight Mushroom Pasta

*Kenji López-Alt's deeply browned mushrooms with short pasta in a loose, glossy sauce of stock, a little cream and butter, finished with pecorino, lemon and parsley. About 30 minutes.*

<!-- Source: https://frienji.kenjilopezalt.com/posts/easy-weeknight-169276035 -->

## Ingredients

- 450 g mixed mushrooms (1 lb), such as the co-op's variety pack, maitake, shiitake or oyster; small ones whole, big ones cut bite-size
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more as needed
- 1 sprig thyme or rosemary (optional)
- 1 small shallot, finely minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 120 g chicken stock (½ cup)
- 60 g heavy cream (¼ cup)
- 30 g unsalted butter (2 tbsp)
- 450 g rigatoni or other short pasta (1 lb), or fresh casarecce
- 55 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated (about 1 cup), plus more at the table
- 1 small handful parsley or chives (¼ cup), minced

## Steps

1. **Boil the water.** Bring a big pot of well-salted water to a boil for the pasta. *(timer: 10 min)*
2. **Brown the mushrooms hard.** Heat the oil in a wok or 12-inch skillet over high heat until it shimmers. Add the mushrooms and herb sprig and season. Toss now and then until they've given up their water and are deeply browned; add a splash more oil if the pan goes dry first. Browning first gives them much deeper flavor than simmering them in the sauce. *(timer: 6–10 min)*
3. **Aromatics.** Add the shallot and toss for 30 seconds, then the garlic for 15 seconds. *(timer: 45 sec)*
4. **Build the sauce.** Toss in the lemon juice and soy sauce. Add the stock, cream and butter. Simmer hard until it reduces a little and looks saucy, then turn the heat to low. *(timer: 2–3 min)*
5. **Cook the pasta.** Cook it 1–2 minutes short of the package time. Scoop out 350 g (1½ cups) of pasta water before draining. *(timer: per package, minus 1–2 min)*
6. **Finish in the pan.** Move the pasta straight into the sauce with a spider so some cooking water comes with it. Turn the heat to high and toss hard, adding pasta water a few spoonfuls at a time, until the sauce is glossy and a little looser than you'd think. The starch on the pasta is what makes the sauce creamy. *(timer: 1–2 min)*
7. **Cheese off the heat.** Take the pan off the heat and pull the herb sprig. Toss in the pecorino and parsley until the cheese melts in, adding pasta water if it tightens. Taste for salt, pepper and lemon. Serve right away with more pecorino.

## Notes

Any mushroom works; what matters is giving them enough heat and time to brown. The soy sauce adds savoriness without tasting like soy. A wok gives the most room to brown and toss.

Fresh casarecce (Seven Sisters at the co-op) cooks in about 3 minutes, so drop it in once the sauce is built.

## Without cream

Leave out the cream and use 180 g stock (¾ cup) with the 30 g butter. Lean a little harder on the pasta water and pecorino in steps 6 and 7. The sauce is lighter but still glossy.
