# Italian-Herb Pork Chops with Sautéed Apples and Roasted Delicata

*A one-pan fall weeknight dinner: seared Stamboom chops, a buttery apple and cider pan sauce, and crisp roasted delicata and Brussels sprouts.*

## Ingredients

- 4 Stamboom Italian-herb pork chops (bone-in, about 1 inch thick)
- 2 delicata squash, halved, seeded, sliced into 1/2-inch half-moons
- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 firm apples (Honeycrisp or Jonagold), cored and cut into 1/2-inch wedges
- 1 shallot or small yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- ½ cup apple cider (unfiltered)
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves

## Steps

1. **Prep and temper.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Take the pork chops out of the fridge, pat them very dry, and let them sit while you prep the vegetables. They're already herb-seasoned, so just a light pinch of salt on each side.
2. **Roast the vegetables (25–30 min).** Toss the delicata and Brussels sprouts with 2 tbsp of the olive oil, about 1 tsp of the kosher salt, and the black pepper. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan with the Brussels sprouts cut-side down. Roast for 25–30 minutes total, flipping the squash and shaking the pan at about 12 minutes. They're done when the edges are deeply browned and a fork slides in easily. *(timer: 28 min)*
3. **Sear the chops.** About 10 minutes into the roast, heat the remaining olive oil in a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Sear the chops without moving them until deeply browned, then flip and cook until they reach 140°F at the center (roughly 3–4 minutes per side). Move to a plate and tent loosely; they'll coast to 145°F. *(timer: 8 min)*
4. **Sauté the apples.** Lower the heat to medium. Add 2 tbsp of the butter to the same pan, then the shallot and apples with a pinch of salt. Cook, turning occasionally, until the apples are golden at the edges but still hold their shape. *(timer: 6 min)*
5. **Make the pan sauce.** Pour in the apple cider and cider vinegar, scraping up the fond. Stir in the Dijon mustard and fresh thyme leaves and simmer until slightly syrupy. Turn off the heat and swirl in the last 1 tbsp of butter, plus any juices from the resting chops. *(timer: 3 min)*
6. **Serve.** Plate the chops with the roasted delicata and Brussels sprouts, and spoon the apples and sauce over the pork.

## Notes

Delicata skin is thin enough to eat, so there's no need to peel it. The chops come about two to a pound, so plan on two packs for four people. If the chops are thinner than 3/4 inch, cut the sear to 2–3 minutes per side. If the Brussels sprouts are large, quarter them so they cook in the same time as the squash.
