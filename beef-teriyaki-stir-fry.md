# Beef Teriyaki Stir-Fry

*Velveted sirloin, broccoli, onion and carrots finished with make-ahead Seattle-style teriyaki sauce — under 45 minutes, about 10 at the stove, no heat*

Uses [Seattle-Style Teriyaki Sauce](seattle-style-teriyaki-sauce.md), made ahead.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb sirloin tip steak (or top sirloin), sliced thin across the grain
- 1 tsp baking soda (for the velveting)
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 10 tbsp (about ⅔ cup) Seattle-style teriyaki sauce
- 2 tbsp water, to loosen the sauce
- 12 oz broccoli, cut into small florets, stems peeled and sliced thin
- 1 yellow onion, cut into ½-inch wedges
- 2 carrots, sliced thin on a sharp diagonal
- 4 scallions, greens sliced thin
- 3 tbsp neutral oil, divided
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 cups short-grain rice (uncooked)

## Steps

1. **Velvet the beef.** Toss the beef with the baking soda and 2 teaspoons of water until every slice is coated. Rest 30 minutes at room temperature, then rinse well under cold water, drain and pat very dry — leftover soda tastes metallic. Toss with the cornstarch. *(timer: 30 min)*
2. **Start the rice and prep.** Get the rice going. Cut the broccoli, onion, carrots and scallions and stage everything by the stove. *(timer: 20 min)*
3. **Loosen the sauce.** Straight from the fridge the thickened sauce is stiff. Whisk it with the water until it pours, and leave it out to take the chill off.
4. **Sear the beef in two batches.** Heat a wok or large skillet over the highest heat until it smokes. Add 1 tablespoon of the oil and half the beef in a single layer: 45 seconds undisturbed, 30 seconds tossing, out while still slightly underdone. Repeat with another tablespoon of oil and the rest of the beef. *(timer: 4 min)*
5. **Stir-fry the vegetables.** Add the last tablespoon of oil. Stir-fry the onion and carrots 90 seconds. Add the broccoli and 3 tablespoons of water, cover 90 seconds to steam, then uncover and toss until the water's gone and the broccoli is bright green and crisp-tender. *(timer: 4 min)*
6. **Glaze.** Return the beef and its juices. Pour the sauce around the hot edges of the pan and toss constantly until everything is coated and glossy, 30 to 60 seconds. It's already thickened, so this is just heating through; pull it before the sugar scorches. *(timer: 1 min)*
7. **Serve.** Over the rice, topped with the scallion greens and sesame seeds.

## Notes

Adapted from [Beef and Cabbage Stir-Fry](beef-and-cabbage-stir-fry.md): same velveting and two-batch sear, with the soy-oyster sauce swapped for make-ahead teriyaki and broccoli and onion in place of the cabbage.

If the glaze tightens up too much in the pan, add another splash of water.

Cabbage still works in place of the broccoli: add it with the onion and skip the steaming water.

Timing: start the velveting and the rice about 45 minutes before dinner; the cooking is under ten minutes once the pan is hot.
