# Latkes with Jonagold Applesauce and Fried Eggs

*Chef John's (Food Wishes) method: grated russet and onion wrung dry and bound with the potato's own starch, fried crisp in shallow oil. The applesauce simmers while you grate. About 50 minutes, 16 latkes.*

## Ingredients

**Applesauce**

- 2 lb Jonagold apples (5–6), peeled, cored, cut into chunks
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Pinch of kosher salt
- 1 tbsp sugar, only if needed (Jonagolds are sweet on their own)
- Pinch of cinnamon (optional)

**Latkes**

- 2 lb russet potatoes, peeled
- 1 medium yellow onion, peeled
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour (or matzo meal)
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- About 1 cup neutral oil, for shallow frying

**To serve**

- 4 eggs, for frying, plus a little butter
- Sour cream
- Chives or scallions, sliced thin

## Steps

1. **Start the applesauce.** Apples, water, lemon juice and salt in a covered saucepan over medium-low. Cook until the chunks collapse when pressed, stirring once or twice. Mash with a fork for chunky or a whisk for smooth, and taste before adding any sugar. Keep warm. *(timer: 18 min)*
2. **Set up.** Heat the oven to 250°F with a wire rack over a sheet pan inside, to hold finished latkes. Pour ¼ inch of oil into a 12-inch cast-iron skillet and set it over medium-high.
3. **Grate.** Large holes of a box grater, or the shredding disc of a food processor. Alternate onion with potato as you go; the onion juice slows the browning that turns grated potato gray.
4. **Wring.** Pile the shreds into a clean kitchen towel and twist hard over a bowl until no more liquid comes out. Let the liquid stand 2 minutes, then pour off the water and scrape the white starch from the bottom of the bowl back into the shreds. That starch is the binder; it's why this recipe needs only ¼ cup of flour and still holds together. *(timer: 2 min)*
5. **Mix.** Shreds, starch, eggs, flour, salt and pepper, tossed with your hands until even. Work quickly from here; the mix weeps as it sits.
6. **Fry.** The oil is ready when a shred sizzles on contact (about 350°F). Drop loosely packed ⅓-cup mounds in, four at a time, and flatten each to ½ inch. Fry until the edges are deep golden and lacy, then flip and fry the second side the same. Loose mounds fry crisp all the way through; packed ones stay raw in the middle. Move to the rack in the oven and salt while hot. Repeat in three or four batches, adding oil as needed. *(timer: 4 min 30 sec)* per side.
7. **Fry the eggs.** In a second skillet over medium, cook the 4 eggs sunny-side up in a little butter until the whites are set and the yolks still runny. *(timer: 3 min)*
8. **Serve.** Four latkes each, an egg on top, applesauce and sour cream on the side, chives over everything.

## Notes

Chef John's video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nHZsE7T7hwI

Too-cool oil makes greasy latkes; if a batch soaks instead of sizzles, wait a minute between batches for the oil to recover. Applesauce keeps 3 days. Leftover latkes re-crisp on a rack at 400°F for 8 minutes. Skip the fried eggs and add Stamboom bacon if that's the mood.
