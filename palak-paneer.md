# Palak Paneer (Swasthi's Method)

*The key: sauté rather than blanch the spinach, and don't overcook it.*

Adapted from Swasthi's palak paneer at [Indian Healthy Recipes](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/palak-paneer-recipe-easy-paneer-recipes-step-by-step-pics/) — see the original for her full recipe and step-by-step photos.

## Ingredients

- 5 cups fresh baby spinach, washed and stems removed
- 8 oz paneer, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 3 tbsp ghee or oil, divided
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1½ tsp ginger-garlic paste (equal parts)
- 1 medium tomato, finely chopped
- 1 green chili, slit (adjust to taste)
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¾ tsp salt
- 3 tbsp heavy cream or whole milk
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- ½ tsp lemon juice

## Steps

1. **Sauté the spinach.** Heat 1 tbsp of the ghee or oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the green chili and the spinach in batches, stirring until fully wilted — about 3 minutes. Don't overcook. Remove from heat and cool slightly.
2. **Purée the spinach.** Transfer wilted spinach to a blender and purée until smooth. Set aside. No need to add water — the moisture from the leaves is enough.
3. **Fry the paneer.** In the same pan, heat another tbsp of the ghee or oil over medium-high. Fry paneer cubes in a single layer until golden on two sides, about 2–3 minutes per side. Remove and set aside. (Skip this step if you prefer soft paneer.) *(timer: 4 min)*
4. **Build the masala.** Add the remaining the ghee or oil to the pan. Add the bay leaf and cumin seeds and let them sizzle for 30 seconds. Add the onion and cook over medium heat, stirring often, until deep golden — about 10 minutes. Don't rush this step. *(timer: 10 min)*
5. **Add aromatics and tomato.** Add ginger-garlic paste and stir for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the tomato, ground coriander, ground cumin, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are completely soft and the oil starts to separate, about 5 minutes. *(timer: 5 min)*
6. **Add spinach and simmer.** Pour in the spinach purée and stir to combine. Simmer over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes. Add the cream and stir through. *(timer: 4 min)*
7. **Finish and serve.** Add the fried paneer and garam masala. Crush kasuri methi between your palms and stir in — this is the key finishing flavor. Add lemon juice, taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Use young or baby spinach — mature spinach has more oxalic acid and can taste bitter. Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) is the finishing touch that makes it taste restaurant-style; find it at any Indian grocery store. The paneer fry step is optional but adds great texture.

Side suggestion: jeera aloo (cumin potatoes) — diced potatoes boiled until just tender, then fried in ghee with cumin seeds and turmeric, finished with fresh cilantro.
