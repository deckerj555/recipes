# Miso Glazed Turnips

*Caramelized and deeply savory — the miso glaze turns lacquered and sticky in the pan. Works as a side or over rice.*

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fresh turnips, peeled and cut into 1-inch wedges
- 2 tbsp white or yellow miso paste
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar or honey
- 1 tbsp neutral oil or butter
- 3 tbsp water
- ½ tsp sesame seeds, for garnish
- 1 scallions or chives, thinly sliced

## Steps

1. **Make the glaze.** Whisk together miso, mirin, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar or honey, and water until smooth. Set aside.
2. **Sear the turnips.** Heat oil or butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the turnips cut-side down in a single layer. Cook without moving until golden brown on the first side, about 4 minutes. Flip and brown the other side, another 3 minutes. *(timer: 7 min)*
3. **Glaze.** Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour the miso glaze over the turnips and toss to coat. Let it simmer and reduce, turning the turnips occasionally, until the glaze is thick, sticky, and clinging to the turnips — about 4 to 5 minutes. Watch carefully as miso burns quickly. *(timer: 5 min)*
4. **Serve.** Transfer to a plate and garnish with sesame seeds and scallions. Serve immediately while the glaze is still glossy.

## Notes

White miso is sweeter and more delicate; yellow or red miso will give a stronger, saltier result. If the glaze reduces too fast before the turnips are tender, add a splash more water. Young, fresh turnips won't need parboiling — older, larger turnips benefit from a 3-minute boil first before hitting the pan.

The main thing to watch is the heat when the glaze goes in — miso has a lot of sugar and will scorch fast if you're not paying attention. Low and slow once it's in the pan.
