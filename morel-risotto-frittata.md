# Morel Risotto Frittata

*A rustic, sliceable frittata that rescues leftover risotto — the rice gives it great structure and the morels add depth you wouldn't get from a plain egg cake.*

## Ingredients

- 2 cups leftover morel risotto, cold
- 4 eggs (7 Mile Farm)
- ¼ cup parmesan or pecorino, grated
- 2 tbsp olive oil or butter
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 pinch black pepper

## Steps

1. **Preheat oven.** Heat your oven to 375°F. You'll need an 8 or 9-inch oven-safe skillet — cast iron is ideal.
2. **Beat the eggs.** Whisk together the eggs, the cheese, salt, and pepper until well combined. The cheese adds salt, so taste before adding more.
3. **Combine with risotto.** Add the cold risotto to the egg mixture and stir to combine. The rice will be clumpy — break it up as best you can. The mixture should be thick and cohesive, not liquidy.
4. **Start on the stovetop.** Heat olive oil or butter in your skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Pour in the risotto mixture and press it flat with a spatula into an even layer. Let it cook undisturbed until the edges look set and the bottom is starting to brown — about 4 minutes. Don't rush this step or it'll stick. *(timer: 4 min)*
5. **Finish in the oven.** Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake until the top is fully set and no longer jiggly in the center — about 12 to 15 minutes. If you want a little color on top, run it under the broiler for the last 2 minutes. *(timer: 13 min)*
6. **Rest and serve.** Let it rest in the pan for 5 minutes before slicing — this helps it hold together. Run a spatula around the edges and cut into wedges directly from the pan. Serve warm or at room temperature. *(timer: 5 min)*

## Notes

If your risotto was very dry, add a splash of water or stock to the egg mixture to loosen things up slightly — you want it to just pour into the pan, not need to be scooped. A handful of wilted spinach or chard stirred in before cooking works well if you want to stretch it further or add some green.
