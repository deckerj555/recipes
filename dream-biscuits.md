# Dream Biscuits (ATK Easiest-Ever Biscuits)

*America's Test Kitchen's dropped cream biscuits: no cutting in fat, no rolling, about 40 minutes start to finish. Warm cream is the trick.*

## Ingredients

- 425 g (3 cups) all-purpose flour
- 4 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- 1¼ tsp table salt
- 462 g (2 cups) heavy cream
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted (optional, for brushing)
- vegetable oil spray, for the measuring cup

## Steps

1. **Heat the oven.** Set a rack in the upper-middle position and heat the oven to 450°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment. The dough comes together fast, so get the oven going before anything else.
2. **Whisk the dry ingredients.** In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
3. **Warm the cream.** Microwave the cream just to body temperature, 95–100°F, stirring once halfway; check with a thermometer. Warming melts the solid butterfat, which loosens the dough enough to scoop without adding more cream. *(timer: 1 min 30 sec)*
4. **Mix.** Stir the warm cream into the dry mix just until you have a soft, uniform dough.
5. **Drop.** Coat a ⅓-cup dry measure with oil spray and drop level scoops about 2 inches apart on the sheet (roughly 2½ inches wide and 1¼ inches tall). Re-spray every 3–4 scoops. Nudge any lopsided ones into level cylinders with your fingertips.
6. **Bake.** Bake until the tops are light golden, 10–12 minutes, rotating the sheet halfway. *(timer: 12 min)*
7. **Finish.** Brush the hot biscuits with the melted butter, if using, and serve warm.

## Notes

Don't make up for cold cream by adding more of it: extra cream gives greasy biscuits that spread instead of rising.

Skip the butter brush if serving with sausage gravy or another rich topping.

Leftovers keep in a zipper bag at room temperature up to a day. Reheat at 300°F for 10 minutes.
