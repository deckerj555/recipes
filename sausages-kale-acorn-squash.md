# Italian Herb Sausages, Garlicky Kale & Brown Sugar Acorn Squash

*A one-oven, one-skillet fall dinner: sweet roasted squash with savory sausages and bright, garlicky kale.*

## Ingredients

- 2 acorn squash (about 1.5–2 lb each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cut into 4 pieces
- 4 tbsp packed dark brown sugar
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1½ lb Italian herb sausage links (Stamboom), thawed
- ⅓ cup water
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 lb lacinato kale (2 bunches), stemmed, sliced into ribbons
- ¼ cup chicken or vegetable stock (or water)
- 2 tsp cider vinegar or lemon juice

## Steps

1. **Prep the squash.** Heat the oven to 400°F. Halve the acorn squash from stem to tip and scoop out the seeds. Trim a thin slice off the bottom of each half so it sits level. Score the flesh in a ½-inch-deep crosshatch, brush with half of olive oil, and sprinkle with 1 tsp of the salt.
2. **Roast cut-side down.** Roast the halves cut-side down on a foil-lined sheet pan until the edges start to soften and brown. *(timer: 25 min)*
3. **Flip and fill.** Turn the halves cut-side up. Put a piece of butter and a tablespoon of brown sugar in each cavity, then dust with cinnamon if using, and black pepper. Return to the oven. Start the sausages now. *(timer: 25 min)*
4. **Steam the sausages.** Put the sausages in a 12-inch skillet with the water. Cover and cook over medium heat so they cook through gently without splitting. *(timer: 8 min)*
5. **Brown the sausages.** Uncover and let the water cook off. Add the rest of the olive oil and brown the sausages, turning occasionally, until deeply browned and 160°F inside. Move them to a plate and tent with foil. Around now, baste the squash rims with the melted butter from the cavities. *(timer: 7 min)*
6. **Wilt the kale.** Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic to the sausage fat and stir 30 seconds. Add the kale by handfuls with the stock, tossing until bright green and tender. *(timer: 5 min)*
7. **Finish and serve.** Stir the vinegar or lemon juice and the remaining ½ tsp of salt into the kale. The squash is done when a knife slides through easily and the sugar butter is bubbling. Let it rest 5 minutes, since the butter is molten. Serve each person a squash half with sausages over the kale.

## Notes

Move the frozen Stamboom sausages to the fridge the night before. For deeper caramel on the squash, broil for 1–2 minutes at the end, watching closely. Maple syrup can stand in for the brown sugar. Don't skip the vinegar on the kale: it balances the sweet squash and the rich sausage.
