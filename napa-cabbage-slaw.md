# Napa Cabbage Slaw with Honey-Cider Vinaigrette

*Crunchy vinaigrette slaw — bright, sweet-tart, and mayo-free; a fresh counter to grilled sausage*

## Ingredients

- 1 medium head napa cabbage (about 1.5 lb), halved, cored, sliced into thin ribbons
- 2 medium carrots, coarsely grated or julienned
- 3 spring onions, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro or parsley leaves, roughly chopped
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- ¼ cup olive oil or neutral oil
- ¾ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 small apple (Gala or Zestar), julienned (optional but great)
- 2 tbsp toasted pepitas or roasted hazelnuts, roughly chopped (optional crunch)

## Steps

1. **Salt the cabbage.** Toss the napa cabbage with kosher salt in a large colander set over a bowl and let sit 15–20 minutes — a light salting pulls out excess water so the dressing doesn't dilute. Pat or spin dry. *(timer: 17 min)*
2. **Make the vinaigrette.** In a large bowl, whisk together apple cider vinegar, honey, Dijon mustard, and black pepper. Whisk in olive oil or neutral oil in a steady stream until the dressing is smooth and slightly thickened. Taste — it should be brightly tart with just enough sweetness.
3. **Toss.** Add the cabbage, carrots, spring onions, apple, and fresh cilantro or parsley leaves to the bowl and toss thoroughly until everything is lightly coated. Taste and adjust salt.
4. **Rest and serve.** Let the slaw sit 15–30 minutes at room temperature (or in the fridge) so the flavors marry while the cabbage keeps its snap. Just before serving, toss again and scatter toasted pepitas or roasted hazelnuts over the top. *(timer: 20 min)*

## Notes

Napa wilts faster than green cabbage, so dress it 15–30 minutes ahead at most — it should still have crunch at the table. For a braai-adjacent variation, swap the vinegar to rice vinegar and add a teaspoon of toasted sesame oil. The apple and market shopping both work in your favor this week: Gala and Zestar apples, carrots, spring onions, cilantro, and Soul Seeds pepitas are all on the list (no napa listed, so that's a grocery-store pickup — a green cabbage from Nature's Finest works too, just slice it thinner and let it sit in the dressing longer, 45–60 minutes).
